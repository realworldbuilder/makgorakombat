01 · for every class

Five rules that win fights

  1. Stun, then spend. A stunned fighter cannot block, dodge or parry, and a stun cancels any cast. Your big hit goes in the stun.
  2. Block the stuns thrown by hand. Hold back: Charge, Kidney Shot and Leg Sweep are blows, and on the guard the stun never lands. Hammer of Justice and Frost Nova are spells and go straight through.
  3. Jump the bolts. Frostbolt, Shadow Bolt, Aimed Shot and the rest fly at chest height: jump as one arrives, or block and take a fifth. Spells that strike at once, like Mind Blast or Moonfire, cannot be jumped or blocked.
  4. Hit the caster. Mid-cast they cannot dodge or parry, and each blow sets the bar back half a second, twice at most.
  5. Parry, then punish. After you parry, your next melee ability inside 1.5 seconds is a sure crit.

Keys as on the game's default layout: J attack, K L U your abilities, A D move, W jump. On WoW's keys (C on the roster) it is 1 2 3 4.

Warrior · rage · Varian, Cairne

Charge, swing, Mortal Strike

  1. KCharge
  2. JSwing
  3. LMortal Strike
  4. UExecute

Rage starts at nothing every round. Charge stuns for a second and gives 20; one swing makes it 30 (37 with a two-hander), enough for Mortal Strike, 14 to 19.

  • Play it. Charge from more than 2.5 yards: up close it will not go. Under 20 health, Execute hits for 23 to 31, so keep 20 rage for it.
  • Beat it. Stay inside 2.5 yards, or hold back as they charge: blocked, the stun never lands.

Paladin · mana · create one

Hammer, then Crusader Strike

  1. LHammer of Justice
  2. KCrusader Strike
  3. JSwing
  4. UHoly Light

Hammer of Justice stuns for 2 seconds from 3.5 yards, and it is a spell, so no guard stops it. Crusader Strike, 11 to 14, lands in full on a fighter who cannot block.

  • Play it. Crusader Strike every 4.5 seconds is your bread. Holy Light heals 35: blows only push the cast back, so it always goes off. Save Hammer to stop their big cast.
  • Beat it. Hammer has a 20 second cooldown. When the Holy Light bar starts, a stun or an interrupt wipes it.

Finish→→LJHammer of Wrath

Hunter · focus · Sylvanas

Shoot, strike, leap, shoot

  1. KAimed Shot
  2. KAgain
  3. URaptor Strike
  4. LDisengage
  5. KAimed Shot

Focus starts full, enough for two Aimed Shots at 22 to 26, and comes back 5 a second. When they reach you, Raptor Strike and leap straight back out to shoot again.

  • Play it. Disengage is off the global cooldown, so strike and leap in one breath. Stand still to cast: moving cancels it, and a cancelled shot costs nothing.
  • Beat it. Jump the arrow, or walk in on the cast: mid-cast they cannot dodge. Once Disengage is spent, stay on them for 15 seconds.

Finish←←KJRapid Fire

Rogue · energy · create one

Kidney Shot, strike, strike

  1. LKidney Shot
  2. KSinister Strike
  3. KAgain
  4. UEvasion

Kidney Shot stuns for 2 seconds. Energy starts full and comes back 10 a second, the fastest in the game, so two Sinister Strikes fit in the stun and neither can be blocked.

  • Play it. Keep 25 energy for Kidney Shot. Evasion is off the global cooldown: for 8 seconds you dodge over half of all blows. Spells ignore it.
  • Beat it. Hold back when they close: a blocked Kidney Shot stuns nobody. A rogue has no reach, so fight from range and cast into Evasion.

Finish←→KKJEviscerate

Priest · mana · Tyrande

Shield, then Mind Blast

  1. UShield
  2. KMind Blast
  3. LHoly Nova
  4. KMind Blast

The shield soaks 25 damage, and a blow it soaks whole does not push your cast back, so the 1 second Mind Blast goes off clean: 17 to 20, straight through any guard.

  • Play it. Mind Blast every 4 seconds from 8 yards. When they reach you, Holy Nova hits all round for 9 to 11 and heals you 8.
  • Beat it. Mind Blast cannot be jumped or blocked, so close in. Break the shield first, then hit her mid-cast.

Finish←←LKShadow Word: Death

Death knight · runic power · create one

Icy Touch into Death Strike

  1. KIcy Touch
  2. LDeath Strike
  3. KIcy Touch
  4. UDeath Coil

Icy Touch reaches 4 yards and halves their walk for 3 seconds, so they cannot get away from Death Strike, which hits 13 to 17 and heals you 10. Each gives 15 runic power; at 40, Death Coil.

  • Play it. Runic power only comes from those two, never from swings, and starts empty. Death Strike every 6 seconds wins the long fights.
  • Beat it. Its pressure comes in 6 second waves: hop back after a Death Strike. Death Coil is a bolt, so jump it.

Finish→←LJSoul Reaper

Shaman · mana · Thrall, Vol'jin

Bolt, bolt, Stormstrike

  1. KLightning Bolt
  2. KAgain
  3. UStormstrike
  4. LEarth Shock

Lightning Bolt costs only 15 mana for 19 to 23. When they reach you, Stormstrike hits 15 to 20. Earth Shock is the trump card: it cancels any cast and locks out casting for 2 seconds.

  • Play it. Against a caster, let the bar start, then Shock from up to 5 yards. Against a fighter, swing and Stormstrike.
  • Beat it. Do not start a cast within 5 yards of a ready Earth Shock. It waits 6 seconds once used: cast then.

Finish←→KLThunderstorm

Mage · mana · create one

Freeze, blink, Frostbolt

  1. KFrostbolt
  2. LFrost Nova
  3. UBlink
  4. KFrostbolt

Frostbolt hits 23 to 27 and slows them for 4 seconds. When they arrive, Frost Nova holds them a second and Blink takes you away; slowed, they walk back into the next bolt.

  • Play it. Your swing is weak, so never trade blows. Nova has a 12 second cooldown and Blink 15: use them together.
  • Beat it. Jump the Frostbolt. Wait out the Nova, then go in: without it a mage cannot keep you off.

Finish←←→KDeep Freeze

Warlock · mana · create one

Corruption, bolt, Howl, bolt

  1. LCorruption
  2. KShadow Bolt
  3. UHowl of Terror
  4. KShadow Bolt

Shadow Bolt is the hardest single hit in the game, 31 to 36. Corruption ticks 10 every 3 seconds for 15. Howl sends them running for 2.5 seconds, and a tick does not break the fear: only a blow does.

  • Play it. Corruption first, then Shadow Bolt whenever there is room. Howl when they reach you, and cast as they run. Recasting Corruption restarts it.
  • Beat it. Jump the Shadow Bolt or hit them mid-cast. Howl only reaches arm's length and waits 20 seconds: bait it, then go in.

Finish→←LKDrain Soul

Monk · energy · create one

Sweep, kick, palm

  1. ULeg Sweep
  2. LRising Sun Kick
  3. KTiger Palm
  4. JSwing

Leg Sweep stuns for 2 seconds; Rising Sun Kick (12 to 17) and Tiger Palm both land in it, unblocked, on 100 energy that comes back 10 a second.

  • Play it. Tiger Palm whenever energy is high, but keep 25 for Leg Sweep. Rising Sun Kick every 8 seconds is your big hit.
  • Beat it. Hold back as they close: a blocked Leg Sweep stuns nobody, and then it waits 20 seconds. A monk has no reach, so keep your distance.

Finish→→JKTouch of Death

Druid · mana · Malfurion

Moonfire, Wrath, Rake

  1. LMoonfire
  2. KWrath
  3. KAgain
  4. URake

Moonfire hits at once for 8 to 10, cannot be jumped or blocked, and ticks 5 four times while you cast Wrath, 18 to 21 for only 15 mana. When they reach you, Rake bleeds them too.

  • Play it. Keep both dots ticking: they do their work while you move, cast or block. Recasting restarts one; it does not stack.
  • Beat it. Wrath is a bolt: jump it. The druid's swing is weak, so press in close, where only Rake can answer.

Finish←→LKStarfall

Demon hunter · fury · Illidan

Rush, swing, Chaos Strike

  1. LFel Rush
  2. JSwing
  3. KChaos Strike
  4. UBlade Dance

Fury starts empty. Fel Rush dashes 4 yards, hits 9 to 13 and gives 20; one swing makes it 30, and Chaos Strike hits 19 to 25 with no cooldown at all.

  • Play it. Fel Rush is your opener and your fury, every 10 seconds. Blade Dance hits front and back: use it when they jump over you or blink past.
  • Beat it. Hit early, before fury builds. Fel Rush is a blow: hold back to take a fifth, or hop back out of its 4 yards.

Finish←→→UEye Beam

Evoker · mana · create one

Flame, breathe, hover, flame

  1. KLiving Flame
  2. LFire Breath
  3. UHover
  4. KLiving Flame

Living Flame hits 22 to 26 for 15 mana. Fire Breath reaches 3.5 yards, further than any sword, for 18 to 22 and a burn; then Hover leaps you back out, off the global cooldown.

  • Play it. Breathe the moment they step inside 3.5 yards, before they can swing, and Hover away at once. Fire Breath waits 12 seconds; Hover 15.
  • Beat it. Jump the Living Flame. After the Hover, it cannot get away again for 15 seconds: stay on it.
