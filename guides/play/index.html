How to play

Mak'gora Kombat

World of Warcraft, as a fighting game. Free, in Chrome.

Swipe →

01 · get in

Drop your WoW folder on the page

The fighters, their gear, the arenas and the sounds are read from your own install, in the browser.

  • Nothing is uploaded
  • Chrome on a Mac
  • Retail, Forever or Classic

On a Mac it is in Applications. The first open takes a moment; after that it is instant.

02 · choose

Choose your champion

Tyrande, Thrall, Sylvanas, Cairne, Vol'jin and the other legends your install has. Or press the question mark and create your own: any race, any class.

Each class fights with its own kit: an auto-attack and three abilities, paid for in rage, mana, energy or focus.

03 · the ground

Fight somewhere real

Gurubashi Arena, the Crossroads, Thunder Bluff, the Maul: real places, raised from your own map files around the two of you.

  • Best of three
  • 60 sec rounds

04 · the keys

Seven keys

  • ADMove, and W jumps.
  • JAttack. Hold it and you swing every time your weapon is ready.
  • KLUYour three abilities. Hold one to read its tooltip.

Rather play on WoW's own keys? Press C on the roster: arrows to move, 1 to attack, 2 3 4 for the action bar. A gamepad works too.

05 · the fight is WoW's

Swing timers and the dice

1.5sa one-hander's swing timer
2.4sa two-hander's, which hits harder
Rollevery blow can miss, be dodged, parried or crit
1sglobal cooldown, then each ability's own

The strategy is the cooldowns. Spend your big hit when it is ready, not before.

06 · guard and dash

Block, dash, hop back

  • hold ABlock. Hold away from a blow and only a fifth of it gets through. Spells go straight past.
  • DDDash in. Double tap toward them.
  • AAHop back. Time it and the swing misses you.

This is what to do while your abilities cool down.

07 · the finish

Finish the Mak'gora!

Take them to zero and they are dazed. You have 4 seconds to enter your class's fatality, shown on your side.

  • Dishonor!a fatality, or any ability
  • K.O.a plain swing, or the clock
  • Honor restoreda round won untouched

08 · play online

Fight someone real

  1. OPlay Online from the roster, and give a name once.
  2. ↵Find a fight. The next player into the queue fights you.
  3. ↑↓Who's here lists everyone online. Challenge a friend by name.
  4. BThe board ranks tonight's wins and all time.

Only your key presses cross the wire. Each of you plays from your own WoW folder.
