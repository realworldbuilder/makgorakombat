How to play

Warrior

Charge in. Hit hard. Execute.

Swipe →

01 · your kit

Four keys, one bar of rage

  • JAttack. Hold it: you swing every time your weapon is ready.
  • KCharge. The opener.
  • LMortal Strike. The big hit.
  • UExecute. The closer.

Cairne Bloodhoof is a warrior, and so is any champion you CREATE as one. On the game's own keys (C on the roster) it is 1 2 3 4.

02 · rage

Every round starts at zero rage

+17a two-hand swing that lands
+10a one-hand swing
+20Charge
+5every blow you take

Carry a two-hander. Two swings pay for a Mortal Strike.

K ability 1

Charge

Run at them from range. It stuns for a second, gives you 20 rage, and it cannot miss.

  • No cost
  • 15 sec cooldown
  • 2.5 to 9 yards

Too close? It needs a run-up. Back off, then go.

L ability 2

Mortal Strike

Your big hit: 14 to 19 damage, double on a crit, every 6 seconds.

  • 30 rage
  • 6 sec cooldown
  • Melee

Spend it the moment you have 30. Rage sitting in the bar does no damage.

U ability 3

Execute

Only once they are under 20% health. It hits for 23 to 31, more than they have left.

  • 20 rage
  • 6 sec cooldown
  • Under 20% health

When they get low, stop spending. Keep 20 rage for this. It can still be dodged or parried.

03 · the loop

Charge, swing, strike, repeat

  1. KCharge in20
  2. JA swing lands37
  3. LMortal Strike7
  4. JTwo more swings41
  5. LMortal Strike11
  6. UUnder 20%? Execute20+

Rage after each step, with a two-hander. Every blow you take adds 5, so you are often ahead of this.

04 · the finish

Finish the Mak'gora!

At zero health they are dazed, and you have 4 seconds.
