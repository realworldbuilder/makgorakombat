How to play

Hunter

Shoot first. Keep your distance.

Swipe →

01 · your kit

Four keys, one bar of focus

  • JAuto Shot. Hold it: a shot every 2 seconds from up to 8 yards.
  • KAimed Shot. The big shot.
  • LDisengage. The way out.
  • URaptor Strike. For when they reach you.

Sylvanas Windrunner is a hunter, and so is any champion you CREATE as one. On the game's own keys (C on the roster) it is 1 2 3 4.

02 · focus

Focus starts full and refills

100at the start of every round
+5every second, whatever you do
−35Aimed Shot
−15Raptor Strike

A cancelled Aimed Shot costs nothing. Focus is only spent when the arrow goes.

K ability 1

Aimed Shot

Stand still for a second and a half, then hit for 22 to 26 from up to 8 yards away.

  • 35 focus
  • 1.5 sec cast
  • 8 yards

Stand still. Moving or jumping cancels it, and every blow you take sets it back half a second.

L ability 2

Disengage

Leap about 5 yards back. It costs nothing and does not wait for the global cooldown.

  • No cost
  • 15 sec cooldown
  • Off the GCD

Mind the warrior. The gap you open is room for a Charge. Shoot before they do.

U ability 3

Raptor Strike

Up close your bow only throws a punch, 6 to 8 damage. Raptor Strike hits for 14 to 18.

  • 15 focus
  • 6 sec cooldown
  • Melee

Hit, then leave. Raptor Strike, then Disengage straight away.

03 · the loop

Shoot, strike, leap, shoot

  1. KAimed Shot65
  2. KAimed Shot again38
  3. JAuto Shot, focus refills57
  4. UUp close: Raptor Strike42
  5. LDisengage42
  6. KAimed Shot from range15

Focus after each step, about: it comes back 5 a second the whole time, so it is rarely empty for long.

04 · the finish

Finish the Mak'gora!

At zero health they are dazed, and you have 4 seconds.
