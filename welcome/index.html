What a Mak'gora is

The Horde's oldest law, finally playable

A Mak'gora is a duel to the death: two fighters, one ring, nobody steps in until one of them falls. Cairne Bloodhoof fell in one to Garrosh Hellscream. Every player who has spammed /duel outside Orgrimmar has wanted a real one.

World of Warcraft duels end at one health and nobody remembers them. Fighting games have the stakes but not your champions. Mak'gora Kombat puts the game's own characters, gear and animations into a fighting game, rules and all.

Why it feels like the real thing

Every fighter, swing and stage comes from the game

  • Your install is the game

    The fighters, their gear, their animations and the stages are read from the files on your computer. No download, no account, nothing uploaded.

  • The fight math is World of Warcraft's

    Swing timers by weapon, every blow rolled on the hit table (miss, dodge, parry, block, crit), and damage ranges like a weapon's 70 to 131.

  • Every class can win

    Hundreds of computer rounds, every class against every other, tuned the kits until each class wins between 46% and 54% of its rounds.

  • Fight a friend online

    Find a fight in the lobby or challenge a player by name. Each of you plays from your own install, or share one keyboard.

Take their health to nothing and you have four seconds. Enter the sequence and finish the Mak'gora, or settle for a plain K.O.

FAQ

Before you drop your folder

Is it safe to drop my World of Warcraft folder on the page?

Yes. The page reads the files in your browser, on your machine. Nothing from the folder is uploaded, and the page cannot change or delete anything in it.

Do I need a Blizzard account or a subscription?

No login of any kind. You need World of Warcraft installed, because the fighters, their gear and the stages are read from those files.

Which computer and browser does it run on?

It is made for Chrome on a Mac. Other Chromium browsers usually work. Safari and Firefox may not read the folder.

Which versions of World of Warcraft work?

Retail (Midnight), WoW Forever and the classic clients. Each one is its own edition of the ring, with its own champions, stages and login screen. A folder that holds two games asks which one to fight in.

Can I fight a friend?

Yes. Play online opens a lobby where you can find a fight or challenge a player by name. Each of you plays from your own install. Two players can also share one keyboard, or plug in a gamepad.

Can I play my own characters?

Yes. Capture them in game with the Mogshot addon and add ?all to the address. That also opens every playable race.

What are the controls?

Player 1 moves with A D, jumps with W, attacks with J and casts with K L U. Player 2 uses the arrows with , . / and Right Shift. Hover any ability in the game for what it does.

Is it made by Blizzard?

No. It is a free fan game with no ads and no purchases. World of Warcraft and its characters belong to Blizzard Entertainment.

Privacy

Your World of Warcraft files are read in your browser and never sent anywhere. The game keeps your settings, your name for online play and a random player id in your browser's storage. Playing online sends your name, your moves and your install's version to the relay that pairs you with the other player. Report an Issue opens a filled GitHub issue in your own browser, which you can read and change before sending. There are no cookies, no ads and no analytics.

A fan game. World of Warcraft is Blizzard Entertainment's; the fighters are drawn from your own copy of it with the Mogshot engine.
